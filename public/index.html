Bring the markup you already have.

Paste a real prompt from your codebase — tags and all. You get back what was removed, what it cost, the audio, and the server frames verbatim.

How to use this
  1. Paste something real. A live template from your codebase, markup included. Don't tidy it first — the tags are the measurement.
  2. Read the receipt. Characters you sent against characters billed. Every figure says where it came from: the API, or computed here.
  3. Check the inventory. What was removed, and what to use instead where there's a real answer. It says "no direct equivalent" rather than inventing one.
  4. Play it, then switch voices. The voice selector sits with the audio so you can re-run the same text and compare.
  5. Read the wire log if you don't believe a number. Every frame, in protocol order, unedited.

One request per run, against a staging endpoint. Your text is held for the life of the request and is never logged or stored.
